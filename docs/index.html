About Me

She is currently a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science in the Graduate Program in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science at the Institute of Mathematical and Computer Sciences (ICMC) of the University of São Paulo (USP), a CAPES level 7 program. She holds a Master’s degree in Computer Science (2020) from the same program and institution, and a Bachelor’s degree in Systems Engineering (2009). Her research interests lie in Computer Science, with a focus on artificial intelligence, data visualization, and the classification and prediction of multidimensional data. She also works on the mining and processing of sound and image data across various domains, including ecology, environmental studies, and soundscape analysis.
